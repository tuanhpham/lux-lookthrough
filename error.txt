  Page structure

  ▎ "A full-screen cinematic landing page divided into snap sections — each chapter fills at least one viewport. Scrolling jumps chapter by chapter (no partial views), with a custom JS-driven 
  ▎ scroll animation (~1 s easeInOutCubic) instead of native browser snap."

  Between-chapter transition

  ▎ "On chapter change, a full-screen blur veil fades in fast (~0.7 s) over the content — dark semi-transparent overlay with backdrop-filter: blur — then fades out slowly (~3 s) after landing,
  ▎ revealing the new chapter underneath."

  Content reveal inside a chapter

  ▎ "Text and images are hidden with clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0) (fully clipped from below). When a chapter becomes active, the clip wipes upward like a rising curtain, staggered per paragraph
  ▎ (~0.18 s apart). Images follow the same direction. Scrolling back up reverses the clip quickly."

  Counter / splash

  ▎ "A boot splash with a circular progress ring (SVG stroke-dashoffset) counting 0–100% over ~20 s. Each digit animates in an iOS picker-wheel slot machine — new digit drops in from above, old
  ▎ digit exits downward, via clip-path overflow:hidden columns with translateY transitions. After 100%, a radial wipe (mask-image: radial-gradient) expands from the center outward, revealing the
  ▎ page underneath."

  Scroll-up behaviour

  ▎ "Chapters already scrolled past stay revealed. Only the chapter currently visible collapses when scrolling back up through it."
